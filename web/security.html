Security

Who can move the pot?

Only the rules. After a quorum approves, the pot goes to the winner's named, locked payee. Nobody can redirect it on their own: not the organiser, not our relayer, not a thief.

The organiser

Opens the circle and earns a fee capped at 5% of the pot, taken only from discounts. The contract has no owner, no admin, no upgrade and no withdraw function.

Our relayer

It only submits members' signatures and pays the gas. Change the amount, round, payee or discount and the signature fails. It holds gas only, never anyone's money.

A thief with a member's key

A payee change waits the circle's delay, every member sees the new name and address, and the member cancels it with one signature. A pot already closed keeps its payee.

The winner's locked payee, after a quorum approves

The payee is fixed when the auction closes. Approvers see its name and address, then anyone releases, and the pot can go nowhere else.

The payee-lock test, run for real

In the first full circle on Aevum, a change of Ah Ming's payout address was requested. It waited, visible to every member, and Ah Ming cancelled it with one signature before it could take effect. Every member action below was a signature; nobody paid gas.
