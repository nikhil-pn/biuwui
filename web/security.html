安全 Threats, controls and limits

Security

A savings circle fails when someone holds the cash. In Biu Wui nobody does: the contract holds it, the rules can't change, and money only moves along fixed paths to each member's locked payee.

威脅

Threat → control

ThreatControl in the contract
The organiser runs off with the potNo owner, no admin key, no upgrade. Circles are EIP-1167 clones of one verified implementation. The organiser's only income is a fee capped at 5% of the pot, and it comes out of the discount.
A winner takes the pot and stops payingPart of each winner's pot (the circle's security share) is held back, never more than they still owe, and pays their later shares automatically at close.
A stolen key redirects the potPots, refunds and dividends only go to the member's named, locked payee. A change waits the payee delay, is visible to every member, and is cancellable with one signature. The winner's payee is fixed when the auction closes.
A release nobody agreed toRelease needs approvals from more than half of the members (the circle's quorum). Members see the payee's name and address before they approve.
The circle never fillsJoining is a pledge. After the join deadline anyone can cancel an unfilled circle and every pledge is withdrawable in full.
A round stallsAfter the grace period anyone can dissolve; everything left (except dividends earned) is refunded pro rata to net contributions.
A standing order drains a walletExactly one share per round, at most N rounds, an expiry date, spender = this circle only, revocable with one signature.
The relayer changes a signed actionEvery amount, round, payee and discount is inside the signature; any change makes it invalid. Nonces stop replays; the circle's address in every domain stops cross-circle reuse.
Bidding manipulationOnly members who paid this round and haven't won may bid; each bid must beat the best; the discount is capped (at most 40%).
中繼

Relayer rails

Members never send transactions. They sign; the app posts the signature to /api/relay; the relayer pays the gas. It is safe to leave open because:

限

Allow-list

Only the documented Biu Wui functions, only on the factory or on circles the factory confirms with isCircle. Anything else is refused.

試

Simulate first

Every call is simulated; a call that would revert is refused with a plain reason and never costs gas.

時

Short signatures

Authorisation windows and deadlines must be at most one hour ahead, so a leaked signature dies fast.

量

Rate limits

Per IP 30 calls / 10 min, per member 60 calls / day, and a global daily gas cap.

油

Gas only

The relayer key holds only tQDAY for gas, never stablecoins, and can only reach paths that already can't move a member's money anywhere but into their circle or to their own payee.

水

Starter faucet

0.03 tUSD8 once per address, 3 per IP a day, and only to addresses holding less than that.

直說

Limits, stated plainly

  • Testnet only. Biu Wui runs on QDay Aevum testnet with tUSD8, a test stablecoin with no value.
  • No audit yet. The contracts have unit, fuzz, invariant and fork tests, but no external audit.
  • Rate limits are in memory per server instance: fine for one instance, a shared store is needed before real traffic.
  • Names are self-declared, not KYC. A payee name is what the member typed. It helps members spot a change; it does not prove identity.
  • The "try without a wallet" key lives in your browser (localStorage). It is for testing only: anyone with access to this browser can use it.
  • Circles need a little trust. Security covers a winner's later shares, but a member who never won and stops paying simply can't bid; the circle carries on without their share.
