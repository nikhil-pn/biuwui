Reading the QDay Aevum testnet…

Nobody holds the pot

The money sits in a contract with no owner. The organiser opens a circle and earns a capped fee, and that's all.

Sign, never pay gas

Joining, paying, bidding and approving are signatures. Our relayer submits them and pays the gas; it can't change a single detail.

A named, locked payee

Every pot goes to the payee fixed when the auction closes. A change waits, everyone sees it, and one signature cancels it.
