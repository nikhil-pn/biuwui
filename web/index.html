How a round works

Everyone pays the same share each round. Whoever needs the money soonest bids the biggest discount, and that discount is shared by everyone. Five steps, every round, the same for everyone.

  1. 1

    Pay付款

    Each member pays one share before bidding ends: by signature, or by a standing order.

  2. 2

    Bid出標

    Members who paid and haven't won yet bid the discount they accept. A new bid must beat the best one.

  3. 3

    Close結標

    Anyone closes the auction once time is up. The highest bid wins, and the winner's payee is fixed.

  4. 4

    Approve批准

    More than half of the members approve the release. Everyone sees the payee's name and address first.

  5. 5

    Release放款

    Anyone submits it. The pot goes to the locked payee; the discount comes back to all as a dividend.

蓋印

Members sign. They never pay gas.

In a Hong Kong office you stamp your chop to agree. In Biu Wui every action is a signature in your wallet, and our relayer pays the gas to submit it. It cannot change a single signed detail: the amount, the round, the payee or the discount.

Security in detail

The weak point of a savings circle has always been trust. Biu Wui moves the rules into a contract with no owner, no admin key and no upgrade.

RiskWhat the contract does
The organiser runs off with the potThere is no owner or admin key. The organiser only gets a capped fee (at most 5%).
A winner stops payingPart of each winner's pot is held as security and pays their later shares.
A stolen key redirects the potEvery pot goes to a named, locked payee. A change waits a delay, every member sees it, and it can be cancelled with one signature.
The circle never fillsJoining is a pledge. If the circle isn't full by its deadline, every pledge is refunded.
A round stallsAnyone can dissolve the circle; what is left goes back pro rata.
Forgetting to payA standing order: exactly one share per round, at most N rounds, expires, revocable at any time.
Members have no gas tokenEvery action is a signature (EIP-3009, EIP-712, EIP-2612); a relayer submits it.

由來

An old idea, everywhere in Asia

A Biu Wui (標會, Cantonese for "bidding association") is how families and small shops in Hong Kong have saved and borrowed for generations. Each round one member takes the whole pot; whoever needs it soonest bids the biggest discount. The same circle has many names.

標會Biu WuiHong Kong · Cantonese
會HuiMandarin · Taiwan
頼母子TanomoshiJapan
계KyeKorea
₹Chit fundIndia

The weak point was always trust: the organiser holds the cash, a winner stops paying, or the pot reaches the wrong person. Biu Wui keeps the custom and moves the trust into code.
