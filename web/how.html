How it works

Five steps, every round.

Like the 標會 your family already knows. The highlighted step is where the newest running circle on the testnet is right now.

  1. 1

    Pay

    Every member signs a payment for exactly one share, for this round, into this circle. Only the contract holds it. A standing order can pay for you.

  2. 2

    Bid

    Members who paid and haven't won yet bid the discount they accept. The biggest discount wins, capped at 40% of the pot.

  3. 3

    Close

    When bidding ends, anyone closes the auction. With no bids, the earliest member who hasn't won takes the pot. The winner's payee is fixed right here.

  4. 4

    Approve

    A quorum of members signs to approve the release. Each of them sees the payee's name and address first.

  5. 5

    Release

    Anyone submits the release. The pot goes only to the locked payee, minus the discount (shared by every member as a dividend) and the winner's security.

Winners keep paying: part of their pot is held back as security against their later shares and paid back when the circle completes. If a round stalls, anyone can dissolve the circle and what's left goes back pro rata.

  1. A

    Joining is a pledge

    You pay your first share when you join and name your payee. If the circle isn't full by its deadline, every pledge comes back in full. You can leave before it fills.

  2. B

    Standing orders

    Let the circle collect your share: exactly one share a round, at most the rounds you choose, until a date you choose. Revoke it with one signature.

  3. C

    Members never pay gas

    Every move is an EIP-3009, EIP-712 or EIP-2612 signature. Our relayer submits it and pays the gas, and can't change the amount, round, payee or discount.
